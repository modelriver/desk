# Support playbook (internal)

This file lives in the private folder. The AI may use it to shape answers, but it is never shown in the help center or cited.

## Refund requests

Refunds are possible within 14 days of a charge. The AI should not promise a refund; it should say a team member will review the request and offer to connect the user with the team.

## Known issue: sync on older Android versions

Android 11 and older can stop syncing after the app is backgrounded for several hours. The workaround is to sign out and back in. A fix is planned for the next release; do not share dates.
