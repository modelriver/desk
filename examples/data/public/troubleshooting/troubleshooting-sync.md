# Notes are not syncing

If edits on one device do not appear on another, work through these steps.

## Check the sync indicator

The cloud icon in the top bar shows the sync state. A crossed-out cloud means you are offline; changes are kept locally and sync when you reconnect.

## Sign out and back in

Signing out clears the local cache. Unsynced changes are uploaded first, so you will not lose work.

## Still stuck?

Ask in the chat and a team member will take a look. Include your device type and app version (found under **Settings → About**).
