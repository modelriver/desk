# Billing and plans

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Members | Storage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | Up to 3 | 1 GB |
| Team | $8 per member / month | Unlimited | 100 GB |
| Business | $15 per member / month | Unlimited | 1 TB, SSO, audit log |

## How billing works

You are billed monthly on the day you upgraded. Adding a member mid-cycle is prorated. Invoices are emailed to the workspace owner and are also available under **Settings → Billing**.

## Changing or cancelling your plan

Owners can switch plans at any time under **Settings → Billing**. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period. When you cancel, your workspace stays readable for 30 days so you can export your notes.
