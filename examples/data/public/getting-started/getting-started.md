# Getting started with Acme Notes

Acme Notes is a shared notebook for small teams. This guide gets you from sign-up to your first shared notebook in about five minutes.

## Create your account

1. Go to the sign-up page and enter your work email.
2. Confirm the email we send you. The link is valid for 24 hours.
3. Pick a workspace name. You can change it later under **Settings → Workspace**.

## Create your first notebook

Click **New notebook** in the sidebar, give it a name and start typing. Notes save automatically every few seconds.

## Invite your team

Open **Settings → Members** and click **Invite**. Invited people get an email and join with the **Editor** role by default. See [Roles and permissions](/articles/getting-started/your-team/roles-and-permissions) for what each role can do.
