# Roles and permissions

Every member of a workspace has one role.

- **Owner**: everything an Admin can do, plus billing and deleting the workspace. Each workspace has exactly one owner.
- **Admin**: invite and remove members, change roles, manage integrations.
- **Editor**: create, edit and delete notebooks they can access.
- **Viewer**: read and comment only.

## Changing someone's role

Admins and the owner can change roles under **Settings → Members**. Click the role next to a person's name and pick a new one. The change applies immediately.

## Transferring ownership

The owner can transfer ownership to an Admin under **Settings → Workspace → Transfer ownership**. The previous owner becomes an Admin.
