# Exporting your data

You can export a single notebook or the whole workspace at any time, on every plan.

## Export one notebook

Open the notebook, click the **⋯** menu and choose **Export**. Pick **Markdown (.zip)** or **PDF**.

## Export the whole workspace

Owners and Admins can export everything under **Settings → Workspace → Export**. We prepare a zip file and email a download link when it is ready, usually within 15 minutes. The link expires after 7 days.
